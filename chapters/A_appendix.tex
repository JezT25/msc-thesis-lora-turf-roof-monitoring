\section{\texorpdfstring{Bill of Materials}{Bill of Materials}}

The bill of materials lists the main components and materials used to construct one in situ sensor node, which is buried beneath the turf roof. It is organized into PCB assembly components and the additional modules and materials used in the implementation. The base station is not included because it is not buried and uses a different controller, the LOLIN D1 mini. The list serves as a reference for the sensor-node hardware described in this thesis. The PCB was manufactured by JLCPCB, and the electronic components used on it were sourced from LCSC Electronics, a sister company of JLCPCB.

The total cost of the priced components and materials listed in the bill of materials for one sensor node is ISK 11,921.44. Components listed as ``Not priced'' or ``Not populated'' are not included in the total. For D2, either a 1N4001 or 1N4004 diode can be used; both are suitable for the intended application and have the same cost. The choice between the two was based on component availability.

\section{\texorpdfstring{Public ThingSpeak Channels}{Public ThingSpeak Channels}}\label{app:thingspeak-channels}
\markboth{A. Additional Material}{A. Additional Material}

The sensor-node measurements are also available through four publicly accessible ThingSpeak channels maintained under the author account \texttt{jvt6hiis}. Each channel corresponds to one of the four sensor nodes (HW\_1, HW\_3, HW\_5, and HW\_7).

The channels provide access to uploaded environmental measurements, including air temperature, relative humidity, soil temperature, raw soil-moisture sensor readings, and battery voltage. The data can be viewed through the online channel visualizations or exported for further analysis.

The public channel links are provided in Table~\ref{tab:thingspeak-channels} to support transparency and allow readers to inspect the measurements collected by the monitoring system. The archived data exports used for the analysis in this thesis remain the basis of the reported results. The public channels are provided as supplementary access to the experimental data.
